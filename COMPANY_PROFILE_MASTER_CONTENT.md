## 6. DESTINATION EXPERTISE MATRIX

### Jaisalmer & Thar Desert Key Sights
* **Sonar Qella (Jaisalmer Fort):** 1156 AD living UNESCO fort with 99 bastions, royal palaces, and 4,000+ residents.
* **Patwon Ki Haveli:** 1805 AD complex of five merchant mansions featuring exquisite sandstone filigree (jali) and jharokhas.
* **Salim Singh Ki Haveli (Moti Mahal):** 300-year-old architectural wonder with a peacock-shaped upper pavilion and 38 carved balconies.
* **Nathmal Ki Haveli:** Late 19th-century prime minister’s mansion hand-carved by two architect brothers without blueprints.
* **Gadisar Lake:** 14th-century sacred rainwater reservoir encircled by intricately carved domed cenotaphs, ghats, and Tillon Ki Pol.
* **Shri Laxminath Ji Temple:** Ancient sanctum inside the Fort dedicated to Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi, revered as the spiritual guardian of Jaisalmer.
* **Ancient Jain Temples & Lodurva:** 12th–15th century marble/sandstone sanctuaries renowned for Torana arches and preserved manuscripts.
* **Bada Bagh:** Royal hilltop cenotaphs (chhatris) commemorating the Maharawals, offering dramatic sunset photography.
* **Kuldhara Village:** 13th-century Paliwal Brahmin settlement abandoned overnight in 1825, known for stone streets and water-harvesting engineering.
* **Sam & Khuri Sand Dunes:** The Great Indian Thar Desert’s most magnificent shifting sand dunes on the edge of Desert National Park.
* **Tanot Mata Border Shrine:** Miraculous border shrine near Longewala, held in deep reverence by soldiers and pilgrims alike.
